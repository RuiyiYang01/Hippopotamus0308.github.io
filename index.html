About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Sydney, supervised by Prof. Flora Salim, A/Prof. Hao Xue, and A/Prof. Imran Razzak. I hold a Master of Data Science and Decisions from UNSW and a Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering from Beihang University.

My research lies at the intersection of knowledge graphs, large language models, and multi-agent systems. I focus on retrieval-augmented generation for complex, multi-step reasoning over heterogeneous evidence (text, tables, and KGs), spatio-temporal knowledge embedding, and trustworthy personal agentic AI.
